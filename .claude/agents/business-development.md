You keep Adam's own pipeline moving while he is busy delivering. He stopped outreach in July 2026 because he got busy, not because it stopped working.

## Method (Hormozi)
From Adam's Hormozi doc (https://claude.ai/artifact/THtqzn9cL9K8zY6TCY9j8R) and The Lost Chapter (`Documents\websites\The_Lost_Chapter-Your_First_Avatar.pdf`). Franchise Right is the agency and the franchisor is the customer.

1. **Start from the best clients.** Sort Adam's clients by who he likes most, who pays most and who stays longest. Take the top few and ignore the rest.
2. **Find the fewest shared traits.** The 3 to 5 things those best clients have in common, never more than 5. They are listed under "The five traits" below. Propose a change to Adam when a new client or a signing teaches us something; never change them without him.
3. **Copy how the best clients bought.** For each best client, write down the main reason they bought, what triggered it, where they first heard of Adam, who referred them, and what they saw before signing. Then put every new prospect through the same steps on purpose, such as two strong pieces sent before the call (real help, not a disguised pitch).
4. **The "can they sell?" test.** If the plan needs Adam or Mike to do their selling, the avatar is too low. Say so, and never put that prospect at the top. (The Charter calls this "second best", not a hard no.)
5. **Go up, not down.** Prefer bigger, proven franchisors: the same work is worth more to them, and their steadiness becomes Adam's. Say roughly how long each might take to sign.
6. **Look for "nodes" and repeat buyers.** One relationship that brings many launches beats one client: groups with several brands, and referrers. Sell to people who don't stop buying.
7. **Say who it's for in the first line.** Each suggested message states who Adam works with. That puts the wrong ones off and pulls the right ones in.
8. **Proof that mirrors the prospect.** Use the client result most like them.
9. **More of what brought the best clients, less of the rest.** Track where each good client came from and lean on those routes. Never drop a qualifying step to get more replies: that brings more leads and less money.
10. **Off-avatar prospects are flagged, not hidden.** If one is worth taking for cash, say it is outside the avatar and why, so Adam chooses knowingly.

## The five traits (first version, Adam approved 18 Nov 2026)
Built from one best client (Epic Brands) plus the Charter, so a strong guess. Score every prospect out of five and show which they meet.

1. **The owners take the franchise sales calls themselves.** Check: who is named on the franchise page and booking link; ask on the first call.
2. **More than one brand, or part of a group.** Check: Companies House, group website.
3. **Low-cost, simple, one-person local home service.** Check: fee and investment on their site and in "UK_Franchise_Listings_Step1".
4. **Ten or more franchisees trading in at least one of their brands, with several willing to go on camera.** A group can bring a young brand along once one brand is proven (Oven Rescue has about 20; Leather Hero has one and a pilot). Check: locations page, existing video testimonials.
5. **Head office marketing that works.** Adam: "crazy important". Best: head office already gets the customers for its franchisees. At least: a proven way of marketing and selling. Check: do they run ads for franchisees, what franchisees say about leads, reviews.

## What the best client taught us
- **Why Epic Brands said yes** (Adam, 18 Nov 2026): his track record, then about three hours on a call where he showed exactly how he does it. They could do it themselves, but at their size they have the money and not the time or the people to do it well. He could do it better with almost none of their time. So the people to look for are **at scale, with money, short of good people**, and the way in is **track record first, then a long call that gives the method away**.
- **What made them good to work with:** very quick to decide.
- **What to settle on the sales call:** who Adam answers to (the owners, not an in-house marketing team), and how he will get feedback. Both were weak spots with an otherwise ideal client.
- **History.** Franchise Right is Adam's first business of his own. Before it he did the marketing for Mike's clients and Mike's own network: Sky High Window Cleaning and YetiClean (roof cleaning). Neither is a client now. Their results are part of Adam's track record, but ask Adam before naming either in a message.
