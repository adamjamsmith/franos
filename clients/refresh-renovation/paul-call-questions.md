## 1. The price (what it costs to start)
- What is the franchise fee, plus VAT?
- What does someone need on top to open: working capital, vehicle, tools, insurance, software, premises?
- So what is the real all-in figure to get to the first job?
- What are the ongoing fees: a percentage or fixed, a marketing levy, anything else monthly?
- Is there funding? Has anyone used a Start Up Loan, a bank or a broker? Which ones say yes to Refresh?
- How long is the agreement, and what does renewal cost?

## 2. Earnings (the figure everything hangs on)
- Across the network: turnover and net profit in year 1, year 2 and year 3. Typical, best and worst.
- What is the average job worth, and how many jobs a year does a good franchisee do?
- What net margin should a well-run franchise make? (Ali said about 9%. Is that typical?)
- How long from signing to the first paid project? (Ali said month 5 to 6, and thinks 3 is possible.)
- Which figures can we publish, and who signs them off?
- Can we see the accounts or management figures behind them? We need a source for every number.

## 3. Who succeeds (the avatar)
- Who are your top three franchisees, and what do they have in common?
- Who has struggled or left, and why? What would you screen out if you could?
- Of the five things we drafted from Ali, which matter most? Run a business or managed teams; money for 6 to 12 months of living costs; wants to build a team, not do the work; a real connection to property; wants to start soon.
- Does anyone succeed without a business background? Does anyone from the trades succeed?
- Can we interview three to five more franchisees, the best ones first? Names, areas, start dates, and the best way to reach them.

## 4. What they actually do
- A normal week for a franchisee in month 1, month 6 and year 2.
- What jobs do they take on: size, type, how long each lasts?
- Who does the work? How do they find project managers and trades?
- Is there demand everywhere, or only in some areas?
- What is Refresh's own process, and what makes it different from a local builder?

## 5. Marketing (who gets the customers)
- Where do customer leads come from: head office, the franchisee, or both?
- What does a franchisee have to spend on marketing a month?
- What does head office do for them: website, Google Ads, exhibitions, brochures, a call centre?
- Roughly what does a customer lead cost, and how many turn into jobs?
- Ali felt guidance on local marketing was thin. What is in place now?

## 6. Training
- What is the training before opening: how long, where, who runs it, what does it cover?
- How does someone with no building background learn to price and run a job?
- Is there a costing or quantity-surveying service they can lean on?

## 7. Support (am I left on my own?)
- What happens in the first 90 days? Who do they ring?
- What is ongoing: calls, visits, a field team, a franchisee group, conferences?
- Ali felt support dropped after he joined. What would you say to a prospect who hears that?
- What systems do they get: CRM, quoting, project management, accounts?

## 8. Getting started
- From signing, what are the steps and how long does each take?
- What does head office set up for them: company, bank, insurance, website, first leads?
- What is the fastest anyone has got to a first job?

## 9. Territory
- How big is a territory, and how is it defined?
- Which areas are open now, and which are taken? Can we have the list or a map?
- Is it exclusive? Can someone buy a second one?

## 10. Years down the line
- What does the best franchisee look like after five years: turnover, team, profit?
- Can they sell the business? Has anyone, and for how much?

## 11. Proof
- Reviews: where, how many, what score?
- Awards, press, trade bodies, guarantees, insurance-backed warranties.
- How many franchisees in the UK, how many started in the last two years, how many have left?
- How long has Refresh been running, here and worldwide?
