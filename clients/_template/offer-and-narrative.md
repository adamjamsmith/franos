# <Brand>: offer and narrative

**Status:** draft / approved by Adam on <date>. Nothing is produced from this until Adam approves it.

This is the one sheet Adam fills in for a brand. Every asset (prospectus, emails, ads, pages, video) is produced from it, together with the avatar card. If an asset needs something that is not here, the answer is to add it here, not to invent it.

Every figure names its source and says whether the franchisor has approved it for publication.

## 1. The offer in one line
What someone gets, for how much, and what it leads to.

## 2. Who it is for, and who it is not
The 3 to 5 qualifiers from the avatar card, and the people we want to put off.

## 3. The promise and the proof
The result we can stand behind, and what proves it: figures, franchisees, years trading.

| Claim | Figure | Source | Approved to publish? |
|---|---|---|---|

## 4. What it costs and what they get
Fee, total investment, ongoing fees, funding. Training, support, territory.

## 5. The narrative
- **Core message:** the one idea every asset carries.
- **Where they are now:** the situation and the pain.
- **The turn:** why this, why now.
- **The belief to change:** what they think today that stops them, and what they need to believe instead.
- **Whose story carries it:** which franchisees, and the moment in each.

## 6. Objections and honest answers
The unasked questions, with the answer and who says it best.

## 7. The path
What they see, in what order, and the one next step each asset asks for.

## 8. Still missing
What we need before this is complete, who it is waiting on, and since when.
