**How reliable:** low to moderate. Scores rest on each franchisor's own sales pages and on franchise directories, which are often undated and disagree on numbers. Trait 1 is confirmed for nobody. Treat the order as who to look at first. Re-check before acting.

## The five traits
1. Owners take the franchise sales calls themselves
2. More than one brand, or part of a group
3. Low-cost, simple, one-person local home service
4. Ten or more franchisees in at least one brand, with several willing to go on camera
5. Head office marketing that works
