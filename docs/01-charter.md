# franOS Charter (the north star)

Every agent reads this before doing anything. Hormozi is the single source of truth on method.

## Goal
Sign more franchisees like each network's **top 20%** (full-time, invested, running it as a business and making real money), **for less money and in less time.**

## Franchise Right's own goal (Adam, 18 Nov 2026)
The goal above is what clients pay for. This is what it is for. Every agent weighs work against both.

- **£5,000 a month by February 2027. A consistent £10,000 a month by May 2027.** After that, take it as far as it goes.
- **More and better, nothing new** (Hormozi: more, better, new). One thing, franchisee recruitment marketing, for 20 years.
- **Two tracks of income.** Set-up fees: paid on signing, the money Adam controls now. Commission: starts two to three months after a launch, then repeats. The £10,000 comes mostly from commission.
- **Adam's job:** the offer and the narrative for each brand (about five days), reviewing what is produced, testing and improving once live, and finding and signing the right clients.
- **The system's job:** produce everything else from the offer and narrative (`clients/<brand>/offer-and-narrative.md`), and keep track of clients, money and prospects so nothing depends on memory.
- **Control what we control.** Nobody chases a franchisor about sales. The Monday brief asks once a week whether any franchisee has signed.

| The three numbers | Now | Aim |

| Income per month | about £3,000 to £4,500 (set-up fees only) | £5,000 by Feb 2027, £10,000 by May 2027 |

Actual payments are kept in the money log, outside this project.

### The ideal client
It takes three things to sign franchisees: marketing, sales, and happy franchisees. Franchise Right brings the marketing, so the right client already has the other two.

- **Must have:** franchisees who are happy and doing well; around ten or more, established and proven.
- **Must have:** someone who can sell it and has sold it before.
- **Best:** owns more than one brand (Epic Brands). One relationship, several launches.
- **Good sign:** simple to run, anyone could do it; local services ideally.
- **Second best:** has the happy franchisees but needs the sales too. Mike can sell, but do not lean on it. Local Treasures sits here.
- **Warning sign:** no proven sales and few franchisees.

## What we are learning, for each brand
1. **Who** that person is: the avatar, described by 3–5 qualifiers at most.
2. **What they did:** which information, in what order, over what time, gets them to sign. That's the path.

Results come from **who they are + what they did.** Keep what moves ideal people forward and cut everything else.

## Principles (from Hormozi)
- Speak only to the avatar. State the requirements up front and put the wrong people off.
- Testimonials mirror the avatar: Meta finds people who look like the person in the ad.
- Quality over quantity. A higher cost per lead is fine if the calls are better.
- Make the best path happen on purpose (e.g. long-form content before the call).
- Train Meta on the most valuable event we can get enough of.
- Small numbers: treat findings as "strong guess, keep testing", pool anonymised data across brands, and watch early signs.
